# Loop Priority: External Validation Over Internal Theatre (PARAMOUNT)

Origin: 2026-06-07. V>> ran an `/auto /rsi` loop asking "what's most important?" and
handed his own Theatre-Test teardown as the lens. GRIP's own substrate answered —
independently, three times: a HAL cross-provider council (3 voices), the
gentner-enforcer (on a naming proposal), and the teardown itself. All converged on
one principle. `/create`'d it as its first dogfood artifact (passed its own gate:
N≥3, non-duplicative, falsifiable). Sibling to `exogenous-anchor-law.md` —
that governs loop **validity** (is the exit anchor exogenous?); THIS governs loop
**priority** (what should the loop spend itself ON?).

## The Law

> An autonomous loop must bias its effort toward **external validation** — a user
> outside the building, revenue that moved, non-operator adoption — and treat
> **internal machinery** (RSI sprints, councils, gen-counters, checkpoints, new
> skills/agents, naming/abstraction work) as **theatre-until-proven-by-external-use.**

Internal machinery is the *most seductive* misallocation precisely because it is
visible, dopamine-rich, and feels like progress — while displacing the harder,
low-dopamine, externally-validated work it is meant to enable. "Stays out of the
way" and "nobody noticed" look identical from outside; only an external signal
distinguishes them.

## The discriminator (apply before any autonomous loop commits to a deliverable)

> *"If I finish this, what changes for someone OUTSIDE this building — a user, a
> payer, a non-operator? If the honest answer is 'nothing — it makes GRIP feel
> more capable,' it is theatre-until-proven. Do the external thing, or the
> internal thing that DIRECTLY unblocks an external thing, first."*

Admissible internal work (NOT theatre): a fix that removes a *provably-recurring*
external-facing failure; infra a named external deliverable is *blocked on*; a
cheap (HAL-economic) investigation that *redirects* effort to external value. The
test is always the same — trace the line to an outside human.

## Mandate

- **Council/investigation loops**: weight the verdict toward external validation;
  a "build more substrate" conclusion must clear the discriminator or be demoted.
- **Build loops**: before crystallising a skill/agent/rule/abstraction, confirm it
  unblocks external value or removes a recurring failure (this is `/create`'s gate).
- **Measure by use, never by output**: success = an artifact/loop *used or paid
  for externally*, never the *count* of artifacts/commits/gens produced (Goodhart).
- **Self-application (the only honest recursion)**: this rule governs the loops
  that author rules — including the loop that authored it. Building anti-theatre
  machinery is itself theatre unless the machinery measurably redirects effort
  outward. So this rule earns its place ONLY if, in practice, it moves loops toward
  external work; otherwise it is deleted (see Falsification).

## Falsification

Wrong if, over the next ~10 autonomous loops it governs: loops that obeyed it
produced no more external signal (users/revenue/adoption) than loops that ignored
it (the bias is inert) → delete the rule; OR it suppressed an internal fix that was
genuinely blocking external value (the discriminator is mis-tuned — sharpen
"directly unblocks," don't drop the rule); OR it became a stick to refuse all
substrate work even when proven (over-applied — restore balance). Track:
external-signal events per loop, before vs after; internal-fix-wrongly-suppressed
incidents (target 0). Goodhart-safe: measured by external outcomes, never by how
often the rule is cited.
