# /howto-talk -- talk instead of type

**HOOK.** You can speak to GRIP. Your words wait in the prompt for Enter.

**WORKED EXAMPLE.** Inside a `gg++` session:

```
/voice
```

Claude Code prints `Hold space to record.` Hold Space and speak. Release it, and the words appear in the prompt. Press Enter to send them.

The recording goes to Anthropic under your login, not to this Mac. macOS Dictation is the path that stays on the Mac.

**### Your Turn.** Run `/voice`. Hold Space and say one sentence. **Completion gate:** your words appear in the prompt before Enter.
