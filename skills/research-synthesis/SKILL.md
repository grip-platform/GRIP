# Research Synthesis

Cross-source research aggregation and knowledge synthesis.

## When to Use

- Combining findings from multiple sources
- Literature review and meta-analysis
- Building knowledge base from diverse inputs
- Learning new domains systematically

## Protocol

### Step 1: Source Collection

- Identify relevant sources (papers, docs, code, conversations)
- Assess source quality (CRAAP: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose)
- Tag each source with key claims

### Step 2: Extraction

- Extract key findings per source
- Note methodology and confidence level
- Identify areas of agreement and disagreement

### Step 3: Synthesis

- Find patterns across sources
- Resolve contradictions (explain why sources disagree)
- Build integrated model
- Identify gaps in coverage

### Step 4: Output

- Summary of synthesized knowledge
- Confidence assessment per claim
- Open questions for further research
- Actionable recommendations

## Integration

Used by: learning, research, synthesis modes.
