## What Does NOT Map (Gentner Compliance)

1. **Fibonacci numbers (1,2,3,5,8) do not correspond to W-levels (0,1,2,3,4)**. Different cardinalities, different purposes.
2. **Broly escalation does not follow W-level**. Research (W1) = CLUSTER. Code (W3) = FORK. Broly level reflects parallelism needs.
3. **Mode stacking is not wave transition**. `code+testing` is parallelism within a position, not movement.
4. **Token budgets do not correlate with W-level**. Budget reflects task complexity, not convergence depth.
5. **Descent is not reverse-ascent**. CODIFIED+ (teaching-via-writing) differs causally from CODIFIED (implementing).
6. **48% of modes (13/27) do not map to wave positions**. Structurally healthy — perfect mapping would be over-mapping.

## Falsification Criteria

**Hypothesis**: 14 of 27 modes map to specific W-axis positions via causal correspondence.

**Null hypothesis**: Modes are independent behavioral presets with no structural relationship to convergence depth.

**Falsification**: (1) Any wave-aligned mode works equally well at two different W-levels. (2) Mode sequencing has no effect on converge scores. (3) 48% non-mapping is evidence of honest classification.
